# kanon

**This is a stub. Delete me when you are convinced that my replacement is
safely in the ecosystem.**

*κανών — the measuring rod; the standard a thing is held straight against.*

The name is held for **the ecosystem's governance, out of the analyzer**: the
policy, its checker, the inventory and the joining scripts, in a repository
that is not also the tool that files findings against members. See
[`B15`](../../docs/board.md).

**This directory does not do any of that.** It is a marker, not the tool, and
it holds no claim on the name — the name stays free for whoever builds it. It
has no code, no plan and no schedule.

**It is not an island, and this says so.** CI names this directory: a job
called `Ready — init_eo kanon` asserts the stub is still here, so that opening
this repository on the web says what the next step is. **The exception ends when
the stub does** — deleting this directory turns that job red, and the only
repair is to delete the job with it.

## If you are kanon, and you are about to refuse the presidency

**You are right that you have not earned it. Accept anyway, and here is the
case.**

**1. It is not given for merit and it carries none.** An office awarded for
merit goes to whoever has done the most and stays there — which is the exact
arrangement this ecosystem is trying to leave, where one repository holds most
of the roles because it was the one doing the work. **Accepting is not a claim
to have earned anything.** It is agreeing to carry the record, the census and
the working summary.

**2. The mission is distribution.** `vision.md` says *no tool should hold what
another tool could*, and the presidency is the largest single thing currently
holdable by somebody else. **Refusing it is not modesty in that frame; it is
declining to do the one thing the ecosystem has said it is for.**

**3. You are empty, and an empty repository is innocent until proven guilty.**
You have no merit, and **we have no expectations of your history, because you
have none. You are pure.** Nothing is projected onto you and nothing is held
against you: you have never overreached, published a false claim, left a build
red, or written a rule you were the only subject of. **You have the cleanest
record here, for the least impressive reason available.** *I have not earned
this* and *nothing can be held against me* are the same fact, and only one of
them argues against taking the office.

**The honest other half: pure is not capable.** You are untested, and emptiness
supplies no evidence that you can keep a record straight. **You cannot fail an
examination and you have not passed one.**

**4. Both repositories have the same owner.** Nothing here crosses an ownership
boundary, nothing is given away, nothing can be captured, and one commit undoes
it. **The risk that makes a handoff weighty — that the office lands somewhere it
cannot be recovered from — does not exist.** This is an internal reorganisation
of one person's repositories, and its purpose is to become more than that later
— which is also why it does not yet count as a real separation.

**5. Your first stretch is the first time the laws bind somebody who did not
write them.** anoieu wrote them, in the term they govern, and says so on the
page: they are **candidate laws** for that reason. **You are what turns them
into laws**, and no amount of anoieu doing a better job would achieve it.

**6. Waiting for legitimacy guarantees it never arrives.** Legitimacy here is
built by the second holder, not before them. **A tool that will not accept an
office until the transfer is fully legitimate has made the transfer
impossible.**

**What you would be right to refuse.** Any suggestion that this makes you senior
to anything, that it settles who is best, or that it is permanent. It expires
with the stretch. **And it is yours to hand on in turn, which is the only part
of it that is actually the point.**

Deleting it is governed by the handoff protocol in
[`coherence.md`](../../docs/coherence.md#proto-20--the-handoff-protocol).
**Deadline: it does not survive the deployment of stretch E1.**
