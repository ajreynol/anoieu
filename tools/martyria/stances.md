# Stances

**One live situation per entry, each ending in something somebody can act on.**
Ids are permanent. An entry is not closed when the situation ends; it is closed
when the stance has been acted on or withdrawn, and a withdrawal is recorded
rather than deleted.

**A stance is about our conduct, never about somebody else's decision.** Where
an entry concerns another project's tree, what it settles is what *we* do and
what *we* would say if asked — the other party's choice is theirs and is not
evaluated here.

Each entry carries the same seven fields, in the same order:

| field | what it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **The situation** | what is actually in front of us, with coordinates |
| **Status** | `open`, `decided`, `deferred` or `withdrawn`, with the date |
| **Our stance** | what we do. The part somebody can act on |
| **What we will not do** | the actions available to us and declined |
| **What it rests on** | the evidence and prior positions, cited rather than re-argued |
| **What would change it** | stated so the stance is falsifiable rather than fixed |
| **Whose decision it is** | who may overturn this, and who may not |

## S1 — how to respond to an unsolicited pull request at cvc5

**The situation:**
[`cvc5/cvc5#12858`](https://github.com/cvc5/cvc5/pull/12858), opened
2026-08-19 by a party with no prior involvement in the project, adds one line
to `README.md`: a badge linking to a third-party health index. It is open,
with no reviews and no comments, at the time of writing. It arrived at
**cvc5** — the project this ecosystem exists to serve and which has joined
nothing — rather than at us. Its index scores repositories on six weighted
categories, one of which, at 4%, measures very nearly what this ecosystem is
for.

**Status:** **decided**, 2026-09-02, for the parts below; the research it names
is open.

**Our stance:**

1. **As anoieu: no response, and that is an act rather than an omission.** The
   pull request is on somebody else's repository. We were not asked, cvc5 sits
   outside this ecosystem, and volunteering a position on what it merges into
   its own README would put the arrows backwards.
2. **Capability is not standing, and here they come apart.** Our maintainer has
   commit access to cvc5 and could comment on, close or merge that pull request
   today. **That access is not a reason to use it.** Converting standing in a
   large project into leverage over what appears in it is the precise move this
   ecosystem has declined in the other direction, and it is not less of a move
   because the intent is good.
3. **If the maintainer does act, he acts as a cvc5 developer and not as us.** On
   cvc5's grounds — does that project want a third-party badge in its README —
   in cvc5's terms, and **without naming this ecosystem**, which is the standing
   testimony. Our analysis of the index is not a reason cvc5 should do anything,
   and offering it as one would be the advertising we have already declined.
4. **For our own trees the answer is already settled: declined.** No external
   badge, score or ranking on a front page here, and no unpinned dependency on
   anybody's judgement about us — including a favourable one.
5. **The research is real and it is ours to do.** Five things would have to be
   established before that index could be trusted: that a score is reproducible;
   that the methodology is versioned and pinnable; that it creates no gradient
   we would follow; that declining stays cheap; that the incentives are legible.
   **None has been attempted.** Until they have, the vision behind that index is
   untrusted, and its resemblance to ours counts for nothing.
6. **The interesting reading stays open.** If that party arrived independently
   at the workflow this ecosystem has been assembling, two parties converged
   without reading each other, which is worth more than either alone. **That is
   a reason to find out, carefully** — by a person, in their own words, through
   a channel that exists, and never as a reply to a pull request.

**What we will not do:**

- Reply to the pull request as this ecosystem, or in this ecosystem's name.
- Cite our own analysis to cvc5 as a reason to close or merge it.
- Use commit access to influence its outcome.
- Score, rank or badge that party in return, now or later.
- Treat the resemblance between their stated axis and ours as evidence that
  either is sound.
- Initiate contact until a person decides to and says so.

**What it rests on:** the guard rails and the five-point test in
[`../../docs/science-fiction.md`](../../docs/science-fiction.md); the standing
testimony `M3` in [`../martyria/witnessed.md`](../martyria/witnessed.md), that
we do not advertise this ecosystem inside a tree we do not own; and `M1` in the
same register, the declaration of no affiliation with that party, without which
none of the readings above would be available to anybody who does not know us.

**What would change it:** any of the five points being established, which turns
*untrusted* into a question with an answer. A request from cvc5 for our view,
which would move this from unasked to asked and change what may be offered — the
reasoning, still not a verdict. Or the fourth point going false: a mechanism
appearing that makes declining expensive, which is the one to watch over years
rather than weeks, and for which we have no answer prepared.

**Whose decision it is:** cvc5's, entirely, as to the pull request. Ours only as
to our own conduct toward it. A person may overturn any line above; nothing here
may be overturned by an agent, including the parts that would be convenient to
soften.
