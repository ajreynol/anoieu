# Reports against us

**Where a claim that this ecosystem violated something is recorded, answered and
settled.** Empty today. It exists before there is anything in it, deliberately:
an invitation with no register behind it is a gesture, and the first report
should land somewhere that already has a shape rather than somewhere improvised
in the moment it is least convenient.

## Why this is not the findings ledger

The ledger in [`../../docs/reports/`](../../docs/reports/reports.md) carries
defects **we** found in **somebody else's** files. This is the other direction
and it has never had a channel. That gap is already written down as one:
[`../../docs/coherence.md`](../../docs/coherence.md) records that findings do
not all come from our checks, that some will come from a project telling us
something is wrong with our own record, and that those have no row to hang off.
This is the row.

## What may be reported, and the bound is the honest part

**A violation of something we have written down.** The policy, the refusals, the
guard rails, a stated position, a rule in a register — anything a reporter can
point at, together with the place we broke it.

**Not a violation of ethics in general**, and the reason is not modesty.
[`../zetesis/`](../zetesis/README.md) has not yet taken a standard from outside;
until it has, this ecosystem has no general standard to be measured against, and
inviting reports against one would be inviting an argument about values in which
we would be both party and judge. **The bound is what makes the invitation
real:** you do not have to persuade us of anything, only show us a rule of ours
and a place it did not hold.

**And a report is a bug.** That is the whole framing. It gets an id, a state and
a settling artifact, it is answered rather than defended, and it is not closed
by our saying we disagree — the same discipline the ledger keeps for findings we
send outward, applied to us.

## What we owe a reporter

**An answer, not a defence.** Accepted, disputed with reasons, or not yet
established — and a dispute names what evidence would settle it rather than
restating our position more firmly.

**No retaliation of any kind**, including the quiet kinds: a report does not
affect a tool's footing, its standing, what we say about it elsewhere, or
whether we take its next one seriously.

**The record stays.** An accepted report is not deleted when it is fixed; it is
settled, with the artifact that settled it, and it stays legible. A register of
complaints that empties itself is a register nobody can audit.

Each entry carries the same six fields, in the same order:

| field | what it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **The claim** | what we are said to have done, in the reporter's words where possible |
| **Reported by** | which tool or person, and the date |
| **The rule it names** | the written thing it says we broke, cited |
| **Our answer** | `accepted`, `disputed`, or `not yet established` — with reasons |
| **The settling artifact** | what closed it, or what would |
| **State** | `open`, `settled` or `withdrawn`, with the date |
